Significance & Spiritual Importance of Griha Pravesh Puja

Before moving into a new home, Griha Pravesh Puja is performed according to North Indian Shastra. It removes negative energy, fosters positive cosmic vibrations, and shields the home from unforeseen difficulties. Steps include Dwar Puja, Toran Bandhan, Cow Puja, Milk Boiling ritual, Gauri Ganesh Puja, Kalash Sthapana, Navgraha Puja, Vastu Purush Sthapana, and detailed Agnihotra Havan.

Spiritual Benefits of Conducting This Ceremony:

Executed before relocating into a home, whether owned or rented.
Conducted by experienced North Indian / Bihari pandit who speaks Hindi fluently.
Shubh Muhurat calculated based on the Janma Nakshatra of the owners.
Includes Vastu Shanti, Navagraha Havan, and Lakshmi Kubera blessings.
